{% macro formatting_guide(subject="text", include_footnotes=false) -%}
While entering {{ subject }}, you may use basic BBCode-like markup.
Markup guide
  • Language – use [lang=tag>]text[/lang]
  • Bold – use [b]bold text[/b]
  • Italic – use [i]italic text[/i]
  • Underline – use [u]underlined text[/u]
  • Overline – use [o]overlined text[/o]
  • Strikethrough – use [s]strikethrough text[/s]
  • Small text – use [sm]small text[/sm]
  • Big text – use [xl]big text[/xl]
  • Inline code – use [code]code[/code]
  • Preformatted text – use [pre]preformatted text[/pre]
  • Coloured text – use [c=COLOUR]coloured text[/c].
    Background-coloured text – use [bg=COLOUR]background-coloured text[/bg].
    Available colours:
    • red | red
    • green | green
    • blue | blue
    • yellow | yellow
    • cyan | cyan
    • magenta | magenta
    • white | white
    • black | black
  • General font formatting [font fg=<colour> bg=<colour> size=<size> family=<font-family> weight=<weight> style=<style>]formatted text[/font].
    General font formatting syntax
    Values may be bare or enclosed in single or double quotes. Attributes may be omitted or given in any order.
    • fg and bg: one of the colours above, or a three- or six-digit hexadecimal colour, with an optional # preceeding it.
    • size: numbers 1-9 or the following keywords:
      Keywords
      1. xxx-small
      2. xx-small
      3. x-small
      4. small
      5. medium
      6. large
      7. x-large
      8. xx-large
      9. xxx-large
    • family: one of the following keywords:
      Keywords
      • serif
      • sans-serif
      • monospace
      • cursive
      • fantasy
      • system-ui
      • ui-serif
      • ui-sans-serif
      • ui-monospace
      • ui-rounded
      • math
      • fangsong
    • weight: a number from 1 to 1000 or one of the following keywords:
      Keywords
      • thin/extra-light = 100
      • ultra-light = 200
      • light = 300
      • normal/regular = 400
      • medium = 500
      • semi-bold/demi-bold = 600
      • bold = 700
      • extra-bold/ultra-bold = 800
      • black/heavy = 900
      • extra-black/ultra-black = 950
    • style: normal, italic, or oblique.
